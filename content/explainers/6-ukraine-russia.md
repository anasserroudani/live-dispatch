# Ukraine & Russia

**Assume zero prior knowledge. This is the great-power conflict everything else orbits.**

Ukraine is a large European country that was part of the Soviet Union until it broke up in 1991. Since then Ukraine has drifted westward — toward the EU and NATO (the US-led Western military alliance) — which Russia sees as a threat on its own border.

**How we got to war:** in 2014 Russia seized Crimea (a Ukrainian peninsula) and backed separatists in eastern Ukraine. Then in **February 2022**, Russia launched a full-scale invasion aiming to topple Ukraine's government. It failed to take the capital; the war settled into a grinding fight in the east and south.

**Why it connects to everything you follow:**
- **Energy** — Russia is a top oil and gas exporter. The war reshaped Europe's energy map and pushed prices up worldwide (see the [[2-energy-and-oil]] thread).
- **The Sahel** — the same Russian mercenary force fighting in Ukraine, **Wagner / Africa Corps**, operates in Mali and Niger. One actor, two continents (see [[9-wagner-africa-corps]]).
- **Great-power rivalry** — it's the most direct US/Europe vs. Russia contest, with China watching closely (see [[1-great-power-rivalry]]).
- **Food** — Ukraine is a huge grain exporter; the war raised bread prices across Africa and the Middle East.

**The lens:** this is a [[1-great-power-rivalry]] story first, a European story second. Watch energy and weapons flows to read it.
