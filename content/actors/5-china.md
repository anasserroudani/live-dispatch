# China

**What it is:** the world's second economic power, expanding its global influence mainly through trade, lending, and infrastructure rather than military bases.

**What it wants:** secure supply of energy and minerals, export markets, reunification with Taiwan on its terms, and a world less dominated by the US.

**How it operates:** the "Belt and Road" — building ports, railways and mines across Africa and Asia in exchange for influence and resources.

**Allies:** a partnership with Russia; deep economic ties with the Gulf and much of Africa.

**Rivals:** the US (the central 21st-century rivalry); tension with neighbours over Taiwan.

**In these stories:** Africa infrastructure and minerals, US–China trade wars, the global backdrop to the Iran war.
