# The United States

**What it is:** the world's dominant military and economic power, with alliances and bases on every continent.

**What it wants:** keep global sea lanes and energy flowing, back key allies (Israel, Gulf states, Morocco, Ukraine), contain Iran, Russia and China, and avoid costly long wars — a balance that shifts with each administration.

**Allies:** Israel, NATO/Europe, Gulf monarchies, Morocco.

**Rivals:** Iran, Russia, China (as strategic competitors).

**Watch for:** US policy can swing fast with domestic politics (elections, Congress). A "foreign" story is often really a US *domestic* story — see the **US & Europe** tab.

**In these conflicts:** the Iran war, Gaza, Ukraine, Western Sahara (recognised Moroccan sovereignty in 2020), Africa policy.
