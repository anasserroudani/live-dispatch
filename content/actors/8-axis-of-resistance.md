# The Axis of Resistance

**What it is:** not one group, but a **network** of armed movements funded and armed by Iran across the region. Learning it as one actor unlocks several conflicts at once.

**The members:**
- **Hezbollah** (Lebanon) — the most powerful; a political party *and* a militia stronger than the Lebanese army.
- **Hamas** (Gaza) — Sunni Islamist (an exception to Iran's mostly-Shia axis — proof it's about power, not just sect); governed Gaza from 2007.
- **The Houthis** (Yemen) — control much of Yemen; attack Red Sea shipping and Saudi Arabia.
- **Iraqi militias** — pressure US forces in Iraq.

**What it wants:** to deter and bleed Israel and the US, and extend Iran's reach without Iran fighting directly. The textbook example of [[3-proxy-warfare]].

**Watch for:** when one front flares (Gaza), expect others (Lebanon, Yemen) to move in coordination.

**In these conflicts:** Gaza, Lebanon, Yemen, the Iran war. Sponsor: [[1-iran]].
