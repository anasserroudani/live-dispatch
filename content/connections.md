# How it all connects

You don't have to memorise 40 conflicts. You have to learn **~15 actors** and **~7 threads** — then every story becomes a combination of things you already know.

This is the *horizontal* layer of the app. The **Learn** tab goes *deep* on one conflict at a time (vertical). This tab goes *across* them (horizontal) — showing the shared players and forces that make separate wars part of one board.

**Read it in this order:**

1. **The Threads** — the cross-cutting forces (energy, great-power rivalry, proxy warfare…). Each lists the conflicts it runs through. This is where "everything connects."
2. **The Actors** — the recurring players. Learn what each *wants* and the news starts predicting itself.
3. **The Analyst's Toolkit** — the repeatable method for reading any new story and slotting it into the map.

The habit to build: for any headline, name the **actors**, name the **thread**, and find **one other conflict** that shares them. Three connections, every time.
