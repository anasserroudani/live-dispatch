Before anything else

Habiba,

Today the whole year pauses to celebrate the fact that you were born — and honestly, it should. The world got warmer the day you arrived in it, and it has been warmer every day you've stayed.

I made you this little corner of light because words in a message felt too small. You deserve more than a text. You deserve a hundred of them, and prayers on top, and a day that reminds you exactly how loved you are.

So take your time here. Read every line slowly. Every single one is true.

— with all my heart, your best friend 💛

One hundred and eight of them

Things you need to know

Because you never let anyone say enough good about you — so here they are, all at once.

Duʿāʾ carried for you

Verses I hold over your name — that your Lord fills your year with ease, light, and mercy.

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا
Fa-inna maʿa al-ʿusri yusrā · Inna maʿa al-ʿusri yusrā
So, truly — with hardship comes ease. Truly, with hardship comes ease.
Sūrah Ash-Sharḥ · 94:5–6
مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ ۝ وَلَلْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لَّكَ مِنَ الْأُولَىٰ ۝ وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰ
Mā waddaʿaka rabbuka wa mā qalā · wa lal-ākhiratu khayrun laka mina al-ūlā · wa la-sawfa yuʿṭīka rabbuka fa-tarḍā
Your Lord has not left you, nor forgotten you. What is coming is better for you than what has passed. And your Lord will keep giving to you, until you are pleased.
Sūrah Aḍ-Ḍuḥā · 93:3–5
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fī ad-dunyā ḥasanah, wa fī al-ākhirati ḥasanah, wa qinā ʿadhāba an-nār
Our Lord, give her good in this life and good in the next, and protect her from all harm.
Sūrah Al-Baqarah · 2:201
وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ
Wa man yatawakkal ʿalā Allāhi fa-huwa ḥasbuh
And whoever places their trust in Allah — He is enough for them.
Sūrah Aṭ-Ṭalāq · 65:3
وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ
Wa idhā saʾalaka ʿibādī ʿannī fa-innī qarīb, ujību daʿwata ad-dāʿi idhā daʿān
When My servants ask about Me — I am near. I answer the call of the one who calls on Me.
Sūrah Al-Baqarah · 2:186

Yā Allah — on the day You gave the world Habiba, let this new year be lighter than every year before it.

Fill her heart with peace that no one can take, her days with rizq and open doors, and her nights with rest.

Heal what she hides. Answer the duʿās she makes in secret. Keep the people she loves close, and keep her far from every harm.

Grant her a life so good that when she looks back, she smiles — and a Hereafter better still. Āmīn. 🤍
